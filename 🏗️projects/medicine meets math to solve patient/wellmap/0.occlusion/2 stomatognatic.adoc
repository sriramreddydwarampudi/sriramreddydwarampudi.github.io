= Mastication 

mastication:: a process aimed to breaking down and salivation of food, preparatory to swallowing


Mastication required for cordinated action of elements 
* Salivary gland 
* Tongue 

Infants thier suck because don't have teeth so mastication is absent 

Mastication in adults

. Preparatory phase
The ingested food is positioned by tongue towards chewing side and mandible moves to the same side

. Food contact
This phase the sensory receptors evaluate the apparent viscosity of ingested food and probabake load on masticatory apparatus. it  is characterized by a movementary pause in mastication.

. Crushing phase
The food is crushed by equal activity on both sides.The crushing starts with high velocity and gradually slows down 

. Tooth contact 
The teeth come in contact and signifies the end of crushing phase

. Grinding phase
The food contact becomes unilateral and transgression of mandibular molars across the maxillary counterparts


. Centric occlusion 
The teeth come to a definite and distinct stop
