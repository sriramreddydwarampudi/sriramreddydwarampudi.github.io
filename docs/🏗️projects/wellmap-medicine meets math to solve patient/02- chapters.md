### **Phase 1: Disease Modeling (Theory - Disease → Signs)**

[**1 - Anatomical Abstraction**](step-01%20-%20Anatomical%20Abstraction.md)  
> Model body parts geometrically (e.g., tooth structure, layers).  
   **(Geometry)**

[**2 - Spatial Anchoring**](step-02%20-%20Spatial%20Anchoring.md)  
> Assign spatial coordinates to anatomical structures.  
   **(Coordinate Geometry)**

[**3 - Cross-System Mapping**](step-03%20-%20Cross-System%20Mapping.md)  
> Map relationships across body systems (e.g., tooth to bone to lymphatics).  
   **(Graph Theory)**

[**4 - Establish Pathophysiology Function**](step-04%20-%20Establish%20Pathophysiology%20Function.md)  
> Define how disease alters normal function dynamically.  
   **(Control Theory)**

[**5 - Trace Dysfunction Progression**](step-05%20-%20Trace%20Dysfunction%20Progression.md)  
> Model how pathology spreads across time and systems.  
   **(Causal Inference)**

 [**6 - Integrate Temporal Evolution**](step-06%20-%20Integrate%20Temporal%20Evolution.md)  
> Model how diseases evolve over time (acute → chronic transitions).  
   **(Markov Chains)**

[**7 - Segment Pathology Zones**](step-07%20-%20Segment%20Pathology%20Zones.md)  
> Define fuzzy boundaries between healthy and pathological zones.  
   **(Fuzzy Logic)**

[**8 - Detect Clinical Manifestations**](step-08%20-%20Detect%20Clinical%20Manifestations.md)  
> Map internal dysfunctions to observable signs and symptoms.  
   **(Pattern Recognition)**

---

### **Phase 2: Diagnosis (Reverse Reasoning - Signs → Disease)**

[**9 - Encode Patient Data**](step-09%20-%20Encode%20Patient%20Data.md)  
> Structure signs, symptoms, and findings into logical sets.  
   **(Set Theory)**

[**10 - Handle Data Uncertainty and Incompleteness**](step-10%20-%20Handle%20Data%20Uncertainty%20and%20Incompleteness.md)  
> Account for missing, noisy, or contradictory clinical information.  
   **(Probability Theory)**

  [**11 - Compare with Known Patterns**](step-11%20-%20Compare%20with%20Known%20Patterns.md)  
> Match patient presentation with known disease models.  
   **(Vector Space Model)**

 [**12 - Generate Probabilistic Outcomes**](step-12%20-%20Generate%20Probabilistic%20Outcomes.md)  
> Calculate probabilities for each possible diagnosis.  
   **(Bayesian Inference)**

[**13 - Apply Logical Rules and Heuristic Shortcuts**](step-13%20-%20Apply%20Logical%20Rules%20and%20Heuristic%20Shortcuts.md)  
> Combine formal rules and quick heuristic patterns ("clinical intuition").  
   **(Logic)**

 [**14 - Compute Diagnostic Function**](step-14%20-%20Compute%20Diagnostic%20Function.md)  
> Integrate all data into a final working diagnosis.  
   **(Function Mapping)**

### **Phase 3: Treatment and Dynamic Correction**

[**15 - Initiate Treatment Pathway**](step-15%20-%20Initiate%20Treatment%20Pathway.md)  
> Start standard treatment plans based on diagnosis.  
   **(Algorithm Design)**

  [**16 - Apply Ethical Reasoning**](step-16%20-%20Apply%20Ethical%20Reasoning.md)  
> Ensure patient rights, dignity, informed consent guide decisions.  
   **(Deontic Logic)**

[**17 - Optimize Treatment Plan**](step-17%20-%20Optimize%20Treatment%20Plan.md)  
> Balance effectiveness, risks, side effects, patient desires.  
   **(Optimization Theory)**

[**18 - Handle Multilateral Decisions**](step-18%20-%20Handle%20Multilateral%20Decisions.md)  
> Manage conflicts between options, specialties, or patient wishes.  
   **(Game Theory)**

[**19 - Adapt Plan Dynamically**](step-19%20-%20Adapt%20Plan%20Dynamically.md)  
> Monitor outcomes, modify treatments based on patient response.  
   **(Feedback Loops)**

[**20 - Evaluate Post-Treatment Effects**](step-20%20-%20Evaluate%20Post-Treatment%20Effects.md)  
> Compare final patient state to expected goals.  
   **(Outcome Analysis)**
