Force 

A load or external influence applied to a body that changes or tends to change the position of the body

Types
- compression (squeezing together)
- tension(streching)
- shear force (lateral shift)



Centre of resistance

The point in the object at which the resistance to movement is at the maximum 

If force applied to the centre of resistance, the whole body moves equally in the direction of the force applied 

The centre of resistance depends on
- root morphology 
- number of roots
- level of alveolar bone support 
- root length
