1. **Upper GI Tract (Esophagus + Stomach/Duodenum)**  
   - *Rationale*: Many symptoms (e.g., heartburn, dysphagia, epigastric pain) overlap and require differential diagnosis across these regions.  
   - **Example Conditions**:  
     - GERD (esophagus + stomach)  
     - Dysphagia (esophageal obstruction vs. motility disorder)  
     - Epigastric pain (gastritis vs. peptic ulcer vs. esophageal spasm)  

2. **Small/Large Intestine**  
   - *Rationale*: Symptoms like diarrhea, bloating, or bleeding localize to these regions.  

3. **Hepatobiliary & Pancreas**  
   - *Rationale*: Jaundice, right upper quadrant pain, or steatorrhea point to these organs.  

4. **Functional GI Disorders (Cross-Regional)**  
   - *Rationale*: IBS, functional dyspepsia, and motility disorders often lack clear anatomic boundaries.
