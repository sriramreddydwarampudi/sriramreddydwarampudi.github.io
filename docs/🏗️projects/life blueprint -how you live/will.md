Great question, Sri Ram! These terms are related but have different meanings depending on the context — especially in planning, education, management, and life.

Here’s a clear breakdown:


---

1. Aim

Definition: A broad intention or direction.

Nature: General, abstract.

Example: "I aim to be a good doctor."



---

2. Goal

Definition: A concrete desired outcome, often long-term.

Nature: More specific than an aim.

Example: "My goal is to open a dental clinic in 5 years."



---

3. Objective

Definition: A short-term, measurable step to reach a goal.

Nature: Specific and action-oriented.

Example: "Complete orthodontics training this year."



---

4. Target

Definition: A precise, often quantifiable aim.

Nature: Numerical or time-bound.

Example: "Treat 20 patients per month."



---

5. Mission

Definition: The purpose or reason for existence of a person, organization, or project.

Nature: Foundational, philosophical.

Example: "My mission is to provide affordable dental care to underserved communities."



---

In a Simple Hierarchy:

Mission → Aim → Goal → Objective → Target
