The reason for this kind of numbering in a drum machine is not based on pitch but rather on function, familiarity, and physical layout for quick access and performance. Let’s break it down:

1. Ergonomics / Finger Mapping

Most drum machines (like the Roland TR-series or MPC pads) are designed to be played with fingers or pads.

The most commonly used sounds (like snare, kick, hi-hats) are placed in the center or first few numbers for quick access.


2. Frequency of Use

Sounds used most frequently in patterns (like bass drum, snare, hi-hat) are given priority numbers.

Less-used or effect-based sounds (like claps, cowbells, cymbals) are given higher numbers.


3. Classic Drum Machine Tradition

Many modern drum machines follow the legacy of machines like:

Roland TR-808/909: which had specific pad order.
