## Where to Buy Mini Pupper Parts in India

Below is a guide to sourcing the main Mini Pupper parts from Indian retailers, focusing on Amazon India and Robu.in, as requested. The Mini Pupper Bill of Materials (BOM) includes custom and off-the-shelf parts. For custom parts, you may need to use local machining or 3D printing services.

### **1. Servos**

- **Standard 9g Micro Servo (SG90)**
  - These are widely available and suitable for prototyping, though the original project uses custom servos. For initial builds or educational purposes, SG90s are a good substitute.
  - **Amazon India:** Search for “SG90 9g micro servo” or similar.
  - **Robu.in:** [SG90 9g Micro Servo](https://robu.in/product/sg90-mini-micro-servo-motor/)

### **2. Carbon Fiber Plates & Custom CNC Parts**

- **Carbon Fiber Sheets and CNC Machining**
  - For the body and leg plates, you’ll need carbon fiber sheets cut to size. Some Indian vendors offer CNC services for custom parts.
  - **Bharat Composites:** Offers carbon fiber sheets and custom CNC cutting as per your design files (.igs, .stp, etc.)[4]
  - **Amazon India:** Search for “carbon fiber sheet” for raw material, but machining will need a local service.
