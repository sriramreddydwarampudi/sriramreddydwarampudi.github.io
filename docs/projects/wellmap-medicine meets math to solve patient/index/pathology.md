Pathology is fundamentally the study of what goes wrong in the body. To understand what’s "wrong," it relies on knowing:

Anatomy — what the normal structure is

Physiology — how the structure normally functions


So:

Anatomy gives the layout (e.g., heart has four chambers)

Physiology gives the function (e.g., chambers pump blood in a cycle)

Pathology studies what happens when the structure or function is disrupted (e.g., myocardial infarction disrupts blood flow → tissue death)


In other words:
Pathology = Dysfunctional Anatomy + Abnormal Physiology

It's the bridge that explains disease using the foundations of structure and function.
