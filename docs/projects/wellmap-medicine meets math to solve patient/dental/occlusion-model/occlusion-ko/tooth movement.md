# peridontal ligament 

Pdl ligament serves the function of providing attachment of tooth to the alveolar bone and also detachment from bone so even in between cause movement 

pdl fibers can regenerated


## Compontents of pdl
i. Fibres
- apical fibers
- trans septal fibers
- alveolar crest fibers
- horizontal fibers
- oblique fibers
- inter radicular fibers


ii. Cellular components 
- undifferentiated mesenchymal cells
- fibroblasts,osteoblasts,osteoclasts
- blood vessels 
- nerve endings associated with pain and proprioception
- tissue fluid as shock absorber

Orthodontic treatment is based on fact that it is possible to move the teeth through the alveolar bone by the application of force


Mechanical force first cause pdl change and later bone remodeling takes place to form calcified structure of tooth again in tooth and alveolar bone


light continuous force ->compression of blood vessels + pdl -> blood flow altered -> prostaglandins -> synthesis of cyclic-AMP,activation of ca+ -> metabolic activity -> activation of osteoclasts 

The process by which conversion of mechanical energy into a biological signal affecting a remodelling response to take place is called transduction
