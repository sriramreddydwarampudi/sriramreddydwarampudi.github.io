= claims


* A Color-Coded Odontogram System

A dental charting system where each finding (e.g., caries, fractures, restorations, periodontal conditions) is assigned a unique color for easy identification.


* Coverage of All Dental Specialties

Includes findings from Periodontics, Prosthodontics, Orthodontics, Endodontics, Oral Surgery, and Pedodontics, making it a universal dental charting system.



* Digital and Manual Use

Can be used manually (printed charts) or digitally (software-based odontogram) in clinics and hospitals.



* Integration with Electronic Health Records (EHR)

Compatible with dental software for automated color-based visualization and easy patient record management.



* Better Visualization and Communication

Simplifies diagnosis and patient education by using clear color-coded markings instead of traditional symbols.



* Customizable for Different Practices

Adaptable for general dentistry, pediatric, and geriatric patients, ensuring flexibility in dental practice.



* Scalable for Teaching and Research

Useful for dental institutions, hospitals, and research, providing a standardized charting system for all professionals.
