Verse: क्रीया समयेन बलवद्भिर्नीत्या च विधिना विभूषिता। तस्यैव सिद्धिरभिवृद्धिं प्राप्तं समये समये॥

Transliteration: Kriyā samayena balavadbhir nītyā ca vidhinā vibhūṣitā Tasyaiva siddhir abhivṛddhiṃ prāptaṃ samaye samaye


---

Translation: The action (Kriya) is powered by the right use of time (Kala) and the intensity (effort) applied with the correct method (vīdhī). The success or result (siddhi) of the task grows over time, reaching fruition in due course.
