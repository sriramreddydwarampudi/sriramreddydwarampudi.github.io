If you want to earn royalties or income without relying on ads and without your audience needing to buy anything, then your best options are platforms or systems that use donations, sponsorships, or public funding. Here’s a breakdown for your case:


---

1. Music

Earn through support, not purchases or ads:

Patreon (Free Tier) – let fans support you monthly, even if you offer free music.

Ko-fi / Buy Me a Coffee – people tip you without buying anything.

Fountain – get Bitcoin micro-payments for podcast + music listeners via streaming.



---

2. Software (e.g., Wellmap)

Free tools, earn through community support:

GitHub Sponsors – people sponsor your open-source software.

OpenCollective – great for funding projects transparently.

Liberapay – continuous donations with no fees.



---

3. Books / Philosophy / Knowledge

Free content, paid support:

Substack (Free Tier) – publish articles, get supporter-only tips or donations.

Notion / Obsidian publish + Patreon – share your second brain, get voluntary support.

Medium Partner Program – pay based on reading time (no ads or purchases needed).



---

4. Podcasts / Teaching

Free access, funded by fans:

Spotify + listener support

Anchor + donations (no ads)

PeerTube – host free podcasts, get donations via links.



---

You’re aiming for:

Zero ads

Zero buying
