(a) A ∪ A = A
(b) A ∩ A = A
Associative Laws
(a) (A ∪ B) ∪ C = A ∪ (B ∪ C)
(b) (A ∩ B) ∩ C = A ∩ (B ∩ C)
Commutative Laws
(a) A ∪ B = B ∪ A
(b) A ∩ B = B ∩ A
Distributive Laws
(a) A ∪ (B ∩ C) = (A ∪ B) ∩ (A ∪ C)
(b) A ∩ (B ∪ C) =(A ∩ B) ∪ (A ∩ C)
De Morgan's Laws
(a) (A ∪B)c=Ac∩ Bc
(b) (A ∩B)c=Ac∪ Bc
Identity Laws
(a) A ∪ ∅ = A
(b) A ∪ U = U
(c) A ∩ U =A
(d) A ∩ ∅ = ∅
Complement Laws

(b) A ∩ Ac= ∅
