VDR
This is the vertical dimension when the mandible is in a rest position. The muscles are in a state of minimal contraction, and there is a slight space between the teeth, known as the freeway space.
VDO
This is the vertical dimension when the teeth are in maximum intercuspation, meaning the teeth are fully in contact in a functional position. It is typically 2-4 mm less than the VDR.
VDS 
This is the the vertical dimension when the mandible is in its maximum stretch position, such as during mouth opening or yawning, where the teeth are not in contact.
