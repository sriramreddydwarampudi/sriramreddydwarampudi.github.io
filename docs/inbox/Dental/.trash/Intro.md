
Prosthodontics is 
Specializes in the aesthetic (cosmetic) restoration and rehabilitation of occlusion
by replacement of teeth



Types Prosthetics Treatment- recommended on missing teeth

Complete denture - replacing whole arch -32 or 12 teeth
Removable partial dentures-replacing multiple teeth from 2 to 9 in a arch
Fixed partial dentures(bridges) - replacing 1 or upto 4 missing teeth in a row
Implant - single tooth


Denture- anatomical gingiva
Bridges- supporting tooth
Implant- osseous 


Removable prosthodontics is defined as, "The replacement
of missing teeth and supporting tissues with a prosthesis
designed to be removed by the wearer"

It can be broadly classified as:
• Removable complete denture prosthodontics
• Removable partial denture prosthodontics
- Extracoronal-onlay
- Intracoronal- inlay
