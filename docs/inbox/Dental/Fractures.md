
**Definition**= 

Fractures occur only in bones

Maxillofacial region is complex Bony structure.So,It is divided into 
- Upper third = Frontal bone
- Middle third = Frontal bone to mandible 
- Lower third = mandible 

Fractures of upper third are called as cranial injuries
Middle third and lower third are called maxillofacial skeletal injuries


We treat only maxillofacial skeletal injuries
