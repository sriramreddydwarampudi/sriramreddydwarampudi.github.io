
1. Endo → Perio Lesion (Primary Endodontic, Secondary Periodontal)

Starts with pulpal infection or necrosis due to deep caries, trauma, or fractures.

Bacteria spread through the apical foramen or lateral canals into the periodontium.

Leads to periodontal pocket formation and bone loss around the root apex or lateral surfaces.


Clinical Features

Non-vital tooth (no response to pulp testing).

Deep isolated pocket near the affected tooth.

Periapical radiolucency extending toward the periodontium.

Pain may be more sharp, severe, and localized.


Treatment

Root canal treatment (RCT) first to remove infection.

If necessary, periodontal therapy follows after endo healing.
