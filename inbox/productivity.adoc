

1. Time Allocation

Deep Work (2–4 hours/day): High-focus tasks like problem-solving, coding, writing, or research.

Shallow Work (2–4 hours/day): Emails, meetings, admin tasks, or simple repetitive work.

Breaks & Recovery (1–2 hours/day): Short breaks, exercise, meditation, or relaxation to maintain energy.

Learning & Growth (30 min–1 hour/day): Reading, upskilling, or reviewing past work to improve.


2. Energy Management

Mental Energy: Used for problem-solving, decision-making, and creativity. Best in the morning or after breaks.

Physical Energy: Maintained with good sleep, exercise, and nutrition. Affects focus and stamina.

Emotional Energy: Influenced by stress, motivation, and social interactions. Positive mindset boosts productivity.


3. Focus & Productivity Techniques

Pomodoro Technique: 25-minute focus sessions with 5-minute breaks.

Time Blocking: Scheduling specific times for different types of work.

Eisenhower Matrix: Prioritizing tasks based on urgency and importance.

80/20 Rule (Pareto Principle): 80% of results come from 20% of the effort—focus on high-impact work.
