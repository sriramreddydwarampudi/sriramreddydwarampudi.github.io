    2. Rationale: Many symptoms (e.g., heartburn, dysphagia, epigastric pain) overlap and require differential diagnosis across these regions.

    3. Example Conditions:

      • GERD (esophagus + stomach)
      • Dysphagia (esophageal obstruction vs. motility disorder)
      • Epigastric pain (gastritis vs. peptic ulcer vs. esophageal spasm)

    4. Small/Large Intestine

    5. Rationale: Symptoms like diarrhea, bloating, or bleeding localize to these regions.

    6. Hepatobiliary & Pancreas

    7. Rationale: Jaundice, right upper quadrant pain, or steatorrhea point to these organs.

    8. Functional GI Disorders (Cross-Regional)

    9. Rationale: IBS, functional dyspepsia, and motility disorders often lack clear anatomic boundaries.
